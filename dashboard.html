Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to resolve a complaint?

Most complaints are resolved within 7-15 days depending on the complexity. You can track your complaint status anytime.

Can I provide additional details after filing?

Yes, you can contact our support team via chat or call the helpline number mentioned in your complaint confirmation.

What happens after I report a lost item?

Our team will conduct a search at the train and relevant stations. You will be notified immediately if your item is found.

Is my information secure?

Yes, all your personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We follow strict data protection policies.
